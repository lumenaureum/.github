<p align="center"><b>Your clients trust you with their most cherished moments. Lumen Aureum helps you keep that trust.</b></p>

Lumen Aureum is studio management software for photographers: clients, quotes, agreements, invoices, and proofing
galleries, running on a computer you own, such as a NAS or home server. Client records, photos, backups, and
galleries are encrypted. It is free and open source, with no subscription.

- **How your clients are protected:** [lumenaureum.org/protection.html](https://lumenaureum.org/protection.html)
- **Tour:** [lumenaureum.org](https://lumenaureum.org/)
